How reliable are early delivery labels?

Held-out evaluation on 32,150 fulfilled Brazilian orders purchased April–August 2018. A gradient boosted model predicts late arrival at purchase. Outcomes become visible at delivery.

Estimating model error after 14 days

Mean absolute difference from eventual error, in percentage points (pp). Lower is better. Each eligible week gets equal weight.

Region Weeks Orders Arrived only (pp) Delay corrected (pp)
North 19 494 10.75 9.96
Other 23 16894 3.47 2.13
SP 23 14751 1.30 2.45

Correction helped in the North and Other groups. In São Paulo it nearly doubled the estimation error. Northern estimates cover 494 orders in eligible weeks.

Forecast quality

Average precision was 0.088 versus 0.044 for a constant forecast. Forecast quality remains modest. Training and threshold selection used earlier orders.

This offline replay evaluates a fixed model. The operational value of retraining remains an open question.

Source: Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist
