How does this work in practice?

Who is this for? A delivery analyst who wants to check whether a prediction model stays reliable as real outcomes arrive.

What happens now? You enter an order. The server runs the trained model and saves the prediction. You later record the delivery time, and the dashboard immediately recalculates the results.

What is not connected? There is no courier feed. Orders and delivery outcomes must currently be entered by you. Model health uses daily snapshots; it does not retrain or replace the live model automatically.

Example: An order is flagged as late risk. If its recorded delivery happens after the deadline, that prediction is correct. One correct order is one successful check—not evidence of 100% future accuracy.
